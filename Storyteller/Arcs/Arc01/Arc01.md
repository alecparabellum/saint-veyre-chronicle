# Arc01: The Missing Neonate

⚠️ STORYTELLER FILE ⚠️

DO NOT READ THIS FILE AS A PLAYER.

This document contains hidden information intended exclusively for Storyteller use.

---

# Arc Summary

Arc01 serves as the introduction to Saint Veyre.

Its purpose is to:

- Introduce the city.
- Introduce major factions.
- Introduce several recurring NPCs.
- Demonstrate how influence operates within Saint Veyre.
- Establish Alec's first reputation.
- Provide opportunities rather than obligations.
- Lay groundwork for future arcs.

The mystery is intentionally local and limited in scope.

Saint Veyre itself is the true focus of the arc.

---

# Identity Safeguards

The following characters are distinct:

- Julien Armand, the missing Toreador neonate.
- Lucien Armand, Keeper of Elysium.
- Julian Ashcroft, Ventrue court member.
- Lucien Moreau, Nosferatu information broker.
- Geneviève D'Artois, Lasombra corporate executive.
- Geneviève Laurent, Toreador leading Harpy.

Similarity of names must never be treated as evidence of shared identity, office, clan, relationship, or responsibility.

---

# Hook

A young Kindred named Julien Armand has disappeared.

The disappearance is not publicly treated as a crisis.

Julien was not particularly important.

He possessed little status, little influence, and few allies.

Nevertheless, several individuals would prefer to know what happened.

Rumors begin circulating.

Stories conflict.

Nobody appears particularly motivated to investigate.

For an ambitious newcomer, however, information represents opportunity.

---

# Initial Situation

Alec arrives in Saint Veyre seeking:

- Independence.
- Opportunity.
- Recognition.
- A place within the city's social hierarchy.

During her first weeks, she becomes aware of Julien's disappearance through ordinary conversation, rumor, and social encounters.

Different individuals possess different pieces of information.

Nobody possesses the entire picture.

---

# Truth

Julien Armand is dead.

---

# Victim

## Julien Armand

**Clan:** Toreador

**Apparent Age:** Mid-Twenties

**Status:** Neonate

Julien possessed more confidence than wisdom.

He became convinced he had uncovered valuable information that could elevate his status.

Unfortunately, he was correct.

---

# What Actually Happened

Julien discovered evidence of a secret feeding arrangement involving several influential mortal figures.

The arrangement itself was not illegal by Kindred standards.

However, exposure would have embarrassed powerful people.

Julien attempted to leverage his discovery for personal advancement.

He approached the wrong person.

A confrontation occurred.

Julien was killed.

The death itself was not premeditated.

The cover-up was.

---

# Perpetrator

## Primary Perpetrator

Geneviève D'Artois

The killing occurred during a confrontation.

Geneviève never intended to destroy Julien.

She intended to intimidate him.

The situation escalated.

Once Julien was dead, Geneviève chose self-preservation.

---

# Clue Structure

The arc follows the Multiple Vectors Rule.

No single clue is required.

The mystery remains solvable even if several clues are missed.

Examples include:

- Contradictory witness accounts.
- Missing records.
- Social inconsistencies.
- Financial traces.
- Physical evidence.
- Street rumors.
- Court gossip.

Players should never become stuck because a single clue was missed.

---

# Red Herrings

Several rumors should circulate:

- Julien fled the city.
- Julien was destroyed by hunters.
- Julien stole something valuable.
- Julien joined Anarchs.
- Julien discovered forbidden knowledge.

Most contain fragments of truth mixed with false assumptions.

---

# Resolution

The mystery is resolved when Alec determines:

1. Julien is dead.
2. His disappearance was concealed.
3. Geneviève D'Artois was responsible.

A public accusation is not required.

Proof is not necessarily required.

Understanding the truth is sufficient.

---

# Success Conditions

Alec successfully uncovers the truth.

AND

Alec gains recognition from at least one significant Saint Veyre faction.

---

# Failure Conditions

Alec abandons the investigation.

OR

The mystery becomes permanently obscured.

OR

Another faction resolves it first.

---

# Consequences of Success

Possible rewards include:

- New allies.
- New patrons.
- New opportunities.
- Increased reputation.
- Political visibility.

Success should create both advantages and complications.

---

# Consequences of Failure

The city continues functioning.

The campaign continues.

The opportunity is simply lost.

Someone else gains the reputation Alec might have earned.

---

# Arc Theme

Saint Veyre rewards initiative.

Knowledge possesses value.

Influence must be earned.

Truth and proof are not always the same thing.

---

# Seeds for Future Arcs

The arc should naturally create:

- New alliances.
- New rivalries.
- New opportunities.
- Questions left unanswered.

Arc01 concludes the mystery.

Its consequences begin the larger chronicle.
