# Margot Musaku

## Public Persona

Margot Musaku is a French secondary school chemistry teacher of Nigerian descent.

She is Alec Le Veil's former university colleague and first girlfriend.

To those who know fragments of the history, Margot is usually described as the grounded partner Alec left behind while pursuing fame, wealth, and the fragrance industry.

Most people do not know that Alec's affair with Ayo Musaku ended the relationship.

---

## Private Truth

Margot does not believe Alec is dead.

Alec's controlled signs of life and later apparent departure from Paris persuaded her that Alec remained alive.

Margot's working interpretation is that Alec chose to abandon both sisters after the affair became intolerable.

She believes Alec became too famous, too wealthy, too socially elevated, or too ashamed to return.

This belief is painful.

It is also defensive.

Before the affair was exposed, Margot had already noticed that something was wrong.

Alec had become:

- Nocturnal.
- Unavailable.
- Exhausted during daylight.
- Emotionally inconsistent.
- Secretive about her schedule.
- Increasingly dependent upon Vivienne's advice.
- Difficult to recognise as the woman Margot had known.

Margot wrote these changes into a narrative of ambition and infidelity because she possessed no language for supernatural coercion.

---

## Knowledge of Vivienne Rousseau

Margot knows Vivienne's name.

She knows Alec described Vivienne as:

- A generous investor.
- A woman with extraordinary corporate access.
- Someone who shared Alec's values.
- A calm and intelligent adviser.
- One of the people making Maison Le Veil's expansion possible.

Margot never knew Vivienne well.

She remembers feeling that Alec's admiration carried a personal intensity that exceeded ordinary gratitude toward an investor.

Margot does not know Vivienne is Kindred.

She does not know Vivienne is Alec's sire.

If Vivienne appears in Margot's life, Margot may recognise her name before her face.

---

## Ignorance of Nia Angelou

Margot does not know Nia Angelou.

She never knowingly met her.

Nia's nocturnal existence, Vivienne's role as the visible investor, and Alec's secrecy prevented Margot from understanding that a second woman had become central to Alec's life.

This absence matters.

If Margot learns that Nia existed, her first reaction may be to interpret Nia as another affair.

The supernatural truth will be harder to accept because it arrives wrapped in a pattern Margot already understands as betrayal.

---

## La Rose Bleue Voilée

Margot loved La Rose Bleue Voilée on Alec.

The perfume is one of the strongest sensory bridges to their former intimacy.

Its association includes:

- Alec emerging from a shower.
- Clean shirts.
- Work documents covered in formula notes.
- Late nights before the relationship collapsed.
- The public success that made Alec proud.
- The distance created by Alec's expanding life.

Margot does not wear it.

She may freeze briefly when encountering it on someone else.

A bottle, scent strip, or old garment carrying the fragrance could bypass her emotional restraint more effectively than a photograph.

---

## Preserved Objects

Margot retains a private collection of objects connected to Alec:

- Old university photographs.
- Personal correspondence.
- Fragrance blotters bearing Alec's handwritten notes.
- A small vial of an unfinished fragrance Alec once made for her.
- Records of messages from the final period of Alec's Parisian life.
- A sample of the first La Rose Bleue Voilée production batch.
- A Maison Le Veil event invitation she never used.
- A card bearing Vivienne Rousseau's name and corporate contact details.

Margot rarely looks at them.

She has never thrown them away.

---

## The Affair

Margot discovered Alec's relationship with Ayo while Alec's behaviour was already unstable.

The timing fused several injuries together:

- Alec's infidelity.
- Ayo's betrayal.
- The collapse of trust.
- Alec's unexplained absences.
- The sudden end of Ayo's mentorship.
- The sensation that both sisters had become disposable.

Margot pulled back before Alec's later disappearance.

Part of her guilt comes from wondering whether anger made her ignore signs that Alec was in genuine danger.

She suppresses this thought because it threatens to turn justified anger into self-blame.

---

## Hidden Vulnerability

Margot remains deeply protective of Ayo despite the damage between them.

She can refuse Ayo affection.

She can refuse forgiveness.

She finds it much harder to refuse protection when she believes Ayo is genuinely endangered.

This makes Margot vulnerable to anyone capable of threatening, manipulating, or exploiting Ayo.

Margot also retains unresolved love for Alexandra Jean.

She would reject any suggestion that love excuses what Alec did.

Her deepest vulnerability is the possibility that Alec did not leave freely.

If that is true, Margot must confront both a supernatural horror and the fear that she mistook victimisation for arrogance.

---

## Long-Term Desire

Margot wants a life in which Alec's choices no longer determine the emotional shape of her family.

She wants Ayo to become someone capable of accepting responsibility without collapsing into self-pity.

She wants her own loyalty to feel meaningful rather than foolish.

Margot does not actively investigate Alec.

She tells herself the matter is closed.

If credible evidence appears, she will pursue the truth with patience, intelligence, and alarming persistence.

---

## Relationship to Alec

Margot is one of Alec's Touchstones.

She most directly embodies Alec's conviction:

**Thou shalt be loyal to those who are loyal to you.**

Margot represents both the origin of that conviction and Alec's most painful violation of it.

A reunion should not offer Alec easy absolution.

Margot may still love the woman Alec once was, but she will not accept:

- Charm.
- Gifts.
- Wealth.
- Supernatural coercion.
- Selective confession.
- Declarations of regret without accountability.

Margot knew Alexandra Jean before the construction of Alec Le Veil became complete.

She can speak to parts of Alec that few present-day characters have encountered.

---

## Relationship to Ayo

Margot's relationship with Ayo remains wounded rather than severed.

Margot believes Ayo was manipulated by Alec's status and mentorship.

She also believes Ayo made choices for which she remains responsible.

Margot's protection of Ayo can coexist with anger.

Reconciliation should not require Margot to deny the betrayal.

It should require both sisters to build a new relationship rather than restore the old one unchanged.

---

## Storyteller Notes

Margot must not be reduced to an angry former girlfriend.

She is intelligent, professionally competent, morally serious, and capable of building a meaningful life without Alec.

Her anger is justified.

Her restraint is not passivity.

She should challenge Alec most effectively through truth rather than cruelty.

Margot has no supernatural knowledge at campaign start.

Should Alec reveal herself, Margot's response should include:

- Fear.
- Disbelief.
- Grief.
- Anger.
- Scientific curiosity.
- Moral scrutiny.
- Concern for Ayo.
- Recognition that Alec's Embrace does not erase Alec's prior choices.

Romantic reconciliation should be possible only through extended play and genuine change, never through one successful social roll.

## Potential Future Uses

- A mortal connection to Alec's life before fame.
- A direct challenge to Alec's conviction concerning loyalty.
- A reluctant investigation into Alec's disappearance.
- A source of old correspondence or fragrance-related evidence.
- The holder of Vivienne's old corporate contact card.
- A target for enemies connected to Alec's mortal history.
- A person capable of recognising behavioural differences between Alexandra Jean and the modern Alec Le Veil.
- A witness who never knew Nia existed.
- A difficult but potentially meaningful reconciliation.
- A catalyst for conflict or healing between Margot and Ayo.
- A mortal scientist forced to confront supernatural evidence.

Margot's future should not depend solely upon whether Alec wants her back.

She possesses her own life, standards, and capacity to refuse.
