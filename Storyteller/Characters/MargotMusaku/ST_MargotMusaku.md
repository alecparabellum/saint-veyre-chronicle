# Margot Musaku

## Public Persona

Margot Musaku is a French secondary school chemistry teacher of Nigerian descent.

She is Alec Le Veil's former university colleague and first girlfriend.

To those who know fragments of the history, Margot is usually described as the grounded partner Alec left behind while pursuing fame, wealth, and the fragrance industry.

Most people do not know that Alec's affair with Ayo Musaku ended the relationship.

---

## Private Truth

Margot has never fully accepted the official uncertainty surrounding Alec's disappearance and presumed death.

She understands that Alec had enemies within the fragrance industry, and she remembers enough of Alec's behavior before her disappearance to know that fear had entered her life.

Margot does not suspect vampires.

She does suspect that the public account is incomplete.

She retains a small private collection of objects connected to Alec:

- Old university photographs.
- Personal correspondence.
- Fragrance blotters bearing Alec's handwritten notes.
- A small vial of an unfinished fragrance Alec once made for her.
- Records of messages from the period before Alec disappeared.

Margot rarely looks at them.

She has never thrown them away.

---

## Hidden Vulnerability

Margot remains deeply protective of Ayo despite the damage between them.

She can refuse Ayo affection.

She can refuse forgiveness.

She finds it much harder to refuse Ayo protection when she believes her sister is genuinely endangered.

This makes Margot vulnerable to anyone capable of threatening, manipulating, or exploiting Ayo.

Margot also retains unresolved love for Alexandra Jean, though she would reject any suggestion that love excuses what Alec did.

---

## Long-Term Desire

Margot wants a life in which Alec's choices no longer determine the emotional shape of her family.

She wants the truth about Alec's disappearance.

She wants Ayo to become someone capable of accepting responsibility without collapsing into self-pity.

Most of all, Margot wants to know that her loyalty was not foolish merely because it was betrayed.

---

## Relationship To Alec

Margot is one of Alec's Touchstones.

She most directly embodies Alec's conviction:

**Thou shalt be loyal to those who are loyal to you.**

Margot represents both the origin of that conviction and Alec's most painful violation of it.

A reunion should not offer Alec easy absolution.

Margot may still love the woman Alec once was, but she will not accept charm, gifts, wealth, supernatural coercion, or declarations of regret as substitutes for accountability.

Margot knew Alexandra Jean before the construction of Alec Le Veil became complete.

She can therefore speak to parts of Alec that few present-day characters have ever encountered.

---

## Storyteller Notes

Margot must not be reduced to an angry former girlfriend.

She is intelligent, professionally competent, morally serious, and capable of building a meaningful life without Alec.

Her anger is justified.

Her restraint is not passivity.

She should challenge Alec most effectively through truth rather than cruelty.

Margot has no supernatural knowledge at campaign start.

Should Alec reveal herself, Margot's response should include fear, disbelief, grief, and moral scrutiny. Romantic reconciliation should be possible only through extended play and genuine change, never through one successful social roll.

---

## Potential Future Uses

- A mortal connection to Alec's life before fame.
- A direct challenge to Alec's conviction concerning loyalty.
- An investigation into Alec's disappearance.
- A source of old correspondence or fragrance-related evidence.
- A target for enemies connected to Alec's mortal history.
- A person capable of recognizing behavioral differences between Alexandra Jean and the modern Alec Le Veil.
- A difficult but potentially meaningful reconciliation.
- A catalyst for conflict or healing between Margot and Ayo.

Margot's future should not depend solely upon whether Alec wants her back.

She possesses her own life, standards, and capacity to refuse.
