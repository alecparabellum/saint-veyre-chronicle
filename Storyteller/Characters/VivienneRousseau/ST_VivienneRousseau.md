# Vivienne Rousseau

## Basic Information

**Name:** Vivienne Rousseau  
**Clan:** Ventrue  
**Status:** Established Kindred  
**Primary Base:** France  
**Former Residence:** Saint Veyre for approximately one decade  
**Relationship:** Long-term partner of Nia Angelou  
**Childe:** Alec Le Veil  

Vivienne's exact generation, sire, and mortal birth date remain deliberately undefined until required by the chronicle.

---

## Public Persona

Vivienne is a respected Ventrue known for refinement, influence, discretion, and the patronage of unusually talented people.

She moves comfortably through:

- Finance.
- Luxury industry.
- Corporate law.
- Art patronage.
- Ventrue society.
- Parisian institutions.
- Private political networks.

Vivienne rarely appears hurried.

She cultivates the impression that problems become manageable merely by entering her field of attention.

Among French Kindred, she and Nia Angelou have been regarded as a power couple for decades.

Vivienne possesses greater formal influence.

Nia controls the emotional terms of their relationship.

---

## Relationship with Nia Angelou

Vivienne is profoundly in love with Nia.

The feeling has endured for decades and shows no sign of diminishing.

Their relationship was initially expected to be exclusive.

Nia proved incapable of remaining confined within that structure.

Vivienne resisted the opening of the relationship at first.

She experienced Nia's other lovers and fascinations as disrespect, risk, and loss of control.

Over time, Vivienne realised that she could either accept Nia's nature or lose her.

She also recognised that she remained the constant in Nia's unlife.

Others passed through.

Vivienne remained.

The discovery allowed her jealousy to become more selective and less frantic.

She does not enjoy every attachment Nia forms.

She no longer mistakes every attachment for a threat to their bond.

---

## Balance of the Couple

Vivienne and Nia function because each contains something the other lacks.

Vivienne provides:

- Patience.
- Political judgement.
- Financial power.
- Long-term planning.
- Restraint.
- Institutional memory.
- A willingness to negotiate before destroying.

Nia provides:

- Emotional force.
- Artistic instinct.
- Social magnetism.
- Audacity.
- Appetite.
- The capacity to act before caution turns into paralysis.

Vivienne can be ruthless when necessary.

By Kindred standards, she is also reasonable, calm, and capable of genuine kindness.

Nia is not evil.

She is more selfish, possessive, impulsive, and violent.

Vivienne is the balance that prevents Nia's passions from becoming a trail of permanent disasters.

Nia is the pressure that prevents Vivienne's caution from becoming a mausoleum.

---

## How Alec Entered Their Lives

Nia became obsessed with Le Masque after encountering it on a mortal's warm skin while feeding.

The fragrance reminded her of sunlight upon skin and fabric.

Its effect could not be recreated properly upon Nia's cold body or within the temperature of night.

The limitation transformed appreciation into obsession.

Once Nia discovered the perfumer, obsession with the fragrance became obsession with Alec.

Nia wanted access.

Vivienne made that possible.

Using corporate connections, Vivienne approached Maison Le Veil as an investor.

Her initial purpose was simple:

Give Nia what she wanted without allowing Nia to approach Alec recklessly or expose Kindred existence.

---

## Vivienne's Genuine Attachment

Vivienne did not expect to become emotionally invested in Alec.

She admired:

- Alec's entrepreneurship.
- Her discipline.
- Her ability to make decisions under uncertainty.
- Her instinct for status and presentation.
- Her refusal to confuse elegance with passivity.
- Her capacity to build loyalty around a vision.
- Her survival through class barriers and industry hostility.

Vivienne began to recognise Alec as an unusually strong Ventrue candidate.

Her interest became a mixture of:

- Patronage.
- Mentorship.
- Clan assessment.
- Attraction.
- Protective feeling.
- Pride.
- The desire to shape a successor.

Vivienne framed the attachment as good judgement because that language felt safer than affection.

---

## The Investor

As far as Alec, Margot, and Ayo knew, Vivienne was an exceptional investor.

She offered:

- Capital.
- Introductions.
- Strategic counsel.
- Supply-chain access.
- Retail leverage.
- Protection from hostile contracts.
- Shared values concerning luxury, identity, and long-term control.

Alec spoke of Vivienne with admiration.

Margot and Ayo therefore knew more about Vivienne than they knew about Nia.

They did not know that the investor's first interest had been arranging access for her lover.

---

## Corporate Intervention

During the development of Masquerade, Céleste Reine escalated her corporate sabotage.

Céleste used years of influence to pressure:

- Major fragrance houses.
- Ingredient suppliers.
- Distributors.
- Retailers.
- Investors.
- Contract manufacturers.

The objective was to force Maison Le Veil into delays and damaging reformulations.

Vivienne removed the obstacles.

She used a mixture of:

- Legitimate capital.
- Corporate leverage.
- Ventrue contacts.
- Dominated intermediaries.
- Threats delivered through respectable channels.
- Quiet acquisition of compromised suppliers.
- Social pressure upon decision-makers.

Céleste never understood why every roadblock disappeared.

She believed Vivienne was merely an unusually powerful financier.

She never realised she was fighting a Ventrue.

---

## Masquerade

Nia pushed Alec toward a sweeter, warmer, more decadent evening composition.

Vivienne helped make the business conditions possible.

She also suggested the name:

**Masquerade**

To Vivienne, it was an amused private reference to Camarilla law.

Alec believed the name referred only to Maison Le Veil's mask symbolism.

Vivienne enjoyed the hidden double meaning.

After the Embrace, the joke became one more example of how much of Alec's life had been constructed around truths withheld from her.

---

## The Original Plan

When Vivienne and Nia accepted that Alec was not a temporary fascination, Vivienne proposed an eventual Embrace.

Nia initially resisted.

She loved Alec's warmth, breath, living skin, and the behaviour of fragrance upon her body.

Vivienne argued that mortality would eventually take Alec away and that Alec possessed the qualities required to thrive as Ventrue.

They sought and obtained authorisation from the Prince of Paris.

The intended process was:

1. Alec would be made a ghoul.
2. Kindred existence would be revealed gradually.
3. Vivienne and Nia would educate her for at least one year.
4. The blood bond would provide protection and control.
5. Alec would be tested within controlled conditions.
6. Vivienne would Embrace her once preparation was complete.

The plan involved affection.

It also involved possession.

Neither woman intended to ask Alec whether she wished to belong to their future.

---

## The Assassination Attempt

Céleste Reine ordered Alec's assassination.

The attack occurred at night in an isolated parking area.

Vivienne was nearby because Alec had become a regular part of both women's nights and because Vivienne had grown increasingly concerned about the corporate escalation.

Vivienne intervened against the assassin.

She was too late to prevent Alec from being shot.

The wounds were fatal but did not kill Alec instantly.

Vivienne had minutes.

She Embraced Alec.

The emergency decision saved Alec from final death.

It also forced the future Vivienne had planned before Alec had consented to any part of it.

---

## Immediate Aftermath

Vivienne informed Nia.

Together they concealed:

- The attack.
- The body that should have existed.
- The medical and police evidence.
- Alec's sudden inability to appear by day.
- The disruption to Maison Le Veil.
- The true reason for Alec's reclusion.

The mortal public was encouraged to believe Alec remained alive, had become private, and later left Paris.

Vivienne arranged controlled signs of life through secure communications and corporate representatives.

The concealment protected the Masquerade and gave Alec time to stabilise.

It also deepened the lie separating Alec from Margot and Ayo.

---

## Alec's Anger

Once Alec understood Kindred society, she recognised the full architecture of manipulation around her.

She learned that:

- Vivienne had approached her for Nia.
- Nia had fed from her.
- Presence had shaped her attachment.
- Vivienne had used supernatural power within her business.
- The Prince of Paris had authorised a future Alec knew nothing about.
- Both women intended to ghoul and eventually Embrace her.
- Her consent had been treated as a logistical problem rather than a requirement.

Alec lashed out.

Nia experienced the rejection as ingratitude and loss.

Vivienne understood it as justified.

She convinced Nia to give Alec space.

This was not purely moral clarity.

Vivienne also understood that pressure would make Alec flee permanently.

---

## Strained Cordiality

Alec still required instruction.

Vivienne and Nia taught her:

- Feeding.
- Camarilla law.
- The Masquerade.
- Ventrue customs.
- Political etiquette.
- The dangers of blood bonds.
- The practical realities of nocturnal existence.
- How to manage Maison Le Veil without appearing publicly.
- How to recognise supernatural influence.
- How to survive anger without exposing herself.

The three maintained a functional relationship.

It was never easy.

Alec needed them.

She did not forgive them.

---

## Saint Veyre

Vivienne and Nia once lived in Saint Veyre for approximately a decade.

They retain memories, old contacts, and incomplete obligations there.

Vivienne suggested the city to Alec as a place where she could begin again.

Her reasons were mixed:

- Saint Veyre was distant from the immediate Parisian crisis.
- Vivienne understood its Camarilla culture.
- Alec could build influence without living beneath Vivienne's constant supervision.
- Vivienne could still reach her through old connections if necessary.
- The city might force Alec to become politically self-sufficient.
- Distance might preserve the relationship better than proximity.

Alec accepted the suggestion because it offered both guidance and escape.

---

## Hidden Vulnerability

Vivienne's greatest vulnerability is not simply Alec.

It is the belief that careful planning can make coercion benevolent.

Vivienne genuinely cares.

She genuinely protects.

She also assumes that superior knowledge gives her the right to decide what another person will eventually understand.

Alec's rejection threatens Vivienne's self-image as a reasonable and ethical Ventrue.

If Alec is correct, then Vivienne did not merely save her.

She participated in taking her life before the bullets did.

---

## Long-Term Desire

Vivienne wants Alec to become:

- A capable Ventrue.
- Politically independent.
- Commercially secure.
- Safe from Céleste.
- Strong enough to survive Nia's intensity.
- Close enough that Vivienne does not lose her entirely.

Vivienne wants reconciliation.

She does not yet understand that reconciliation may require accepting a future in which Alec owes her nothing.

---

## Relationship to Nia

Vivienne will protect Nia even when she disapproves of her.

She will restrain Nia when necessary.

She will not tolerate outsiders treating Nia as a disposable liability.

Alec can create genuine conflict between them because Alec's anger toward Nia is justified and Vivienne knows it.

Vivienne's challenge is to protect the woman she has loved for decades without invalidating the childe whose autonomy they both violated.

---

## Storyteller Notes

Vivienne should not be reduced to a secret villain.

She saved Alec's life.

She also planned to control it.

Both are true.

Vivienne's kindness should be real.

Her ruthlessness should be real.

Her love for Nia should not make her foolish, but it should explain why she crossed boundaries she would have condemned in another context.

Her affection for Alec should contain pride, desire, protectiveness, and guilt without resolving neatly into romance or parenthood.
