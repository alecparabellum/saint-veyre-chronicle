# Julien Armand

## Basic Information

**Full Name:** Julien Armand

**Clan:** Toreador

**Apparent Age:** Mid-Twenties

**Status:** Neonate

**Domain:** Saint Veyre

---

## Identity Safeguard

Julien Armand is a distinct character.

He is not:

- Lucien Armand, Keeper of Elysium.
- Julian Ashcroft, Ventrue court member.

The similarity of their names should never be treated as evidence of kinship, office, or shared identity unless future canon explicitly establishes such a connection.

---

## Public Persona

Julien presented himself as an ambitious young Toreador eager to rise above his limited standing.

He possessed confidence, social appetite, and a strong desire to be recognized by more established Kindred.

Many regarded him as clever enough to notice opportunities but not experienced enough to understand all their dangers.

---

## Private Truth

Julien believed information could purchase the status that age and influence had not yet granted him.

He became convinced that he had uncovered something valuable enough to alter his position within Saint Veyre.

His assessment of the information's value was correct.

His assessment of his leverage was not.

---

## Hidden Vulnerability

Julien's ambition exceeded his judgment.

He mistook possession of sensitive information for control over those implicated by it.

He was especially vulnerable to flattery, promises of recognition, and opportunities that appeared to confirm his own importance.

---

## Long-Term Desire

Before his disappearance, Julien wanted to become impossible for Saint Veyre's court to overlook.

He sought recognition, access, and a place among figures whose attention he envied.

## Relationship To Alec

Alec had no established personal relationship with Julien at the beginning of the chronicle.

Her understanding of him should emerge through rumor, records, witnesses, physical evidence, and the interpretations of those who knew or used him.

Julien should not become an exposition device after his disappearance. His character should be reconstructed through incomplete and sometimes contradictory traces.

---

## Storyteller Notes

Julien should feel like a person whose weaknesses contributed to his danger, not someone whose fate made him foolish or disposable.

Different witnesses may remember him as:

- Charming.
- Arrogant.
- Frightened.
- Ambitious.
- Reckless.
- More perceptive than expected.
- Easier to manipulate than he believed.

No single account should define him completely.

---

## Potential Uses

- Conflicting witness testimony.
- Social and financial records.
- Personal correspondence.
- Evidence of ambition and fear.
- A lens into Saint Veyre's treatment of politically insignificant Kindred.
- A reminder that possessing truth is not the same as possessing power.
