# Nia Angelou

## Basic Information

**Name:** Nia Angelou  
**Clan:** Toreador  
**Nationality:** American  
**Apparent Age:** Early Forties  
**Actual Age:** Deliberately flexible; she has been Kindred for several decades  
**Former Occupation:** Fashion designer, costume creative, stylist, and show-business figure  
**Primary Base:** France  
**Long-Term Partner:** Vivienne Rousseau  
**Relationship to Alec:** Former lover, patron, creative influence, and unresolved obsession  

Nia's exact sire, generation, and date of Embrace remain undefined until required by the chronicle.

---

## Public Persona

Nia is a former fashion and show-business creative who withdrew from mortal public life and became a private patron.

Within French Kindred society, she is known as:

- A Toreador of exceptional taste.
- A woman with long connections to fashion, performance, and nightlife.
- A volatile but valuable patron.
- Vivienne Rousseau's long-term partner.
- The emotionally dominant half of an established power couple.
- Someone whose attention can transform a career or consume it.

Nia does not cultivate the image of harmless refinement.

Her charm is warm, direct, and physical.

People frequently understand that she is dangerous before understanding why they still want her attention.

---

## Appearance and Visual Direction

Nia's canonical appearance is based upon the established reference images.

She is a dark-skinned Black woman with:

- A mature face.
- Long oval structure.
- Deep-set dark eyes.
- High cheekbones.
- A broad elegant nose.
- Full lips.
- Long limbs.
- A softly curvaceous, athletic body.
- Enormous natural 4C hair worn in a dense cloud around her head and shoulders.

Her visual grammar combines:

- Ivory.
- White.
- Deep navy.
- Warm walnut.
- Gold.
- Modernist domestic interiors.
- Tropical plants.
- Bare feet.
- Silk and fine knitwear.
- Understated jewellery.
- Intimacy presented without embarrassment.

Nia should not default to gothic black, exaggerated red, or costume-like Toreador styling.

Her danger is more effective when surrounded by daylight colours she can no longer experience.

---

## Mortal Career

Nia worked in clothes design, costume, styling, and show business.

Her talent lay in understanding the relationship between clothing and attention.

She knew how to design garments that changed:

- Posture.
- Movement.
- Social perception.
- Sexual confidence.
- The emotional temperature of a performance.
- The identity a person believed they were allowed to inhabit.

Nia's mortal career made her sensitive to:

- Fabric upon warm skin.
- Sweat absorbed by clothing.
- Stage light.
- Dressing rooms.
- Powder, perfume, and body heat.
- The private transformation before public appearance.

These memories remain central to her Toreador fixation.

---

## Relationship with Vivienne Rousseau

Nia and Vivienne have loved one another for decades.

Vivienne is more politically influential.

Nia controls the relationship.

The relationship became open because Nia could not remain exclusive without experiencing it as confinement.

Vivienne initially suffered.

Nia knew this.

She did not stop.

Over time, Vivienne accepted that she was the constant in Nia's unlife.

Nia's other lovers and fascinations usually passed.

Vivienne remained.

Nia depends upon this constancy more than she admits.

She sometimes tests it through selfish behaviour because being forgiven reassures her that Vivienne has not changed.

This is one of Nia's ugliest habits.

It is also rooted in genuine fear.

---

## Moral Character

Nia is not evil.

She is more selfish and more violent than Vivienne.

She understands other people's feelings very well.

Her failure is not ignorance.

It is the belief that the intensity of her own feeling grants it priority.

Nia is capable of:

- Genuine tenderness.
- Artistic generosity.
- Fierce protection.
- Delight.
- Playfulness.
- Transformative encouragement.
- Brutality.
- Possessiveness.
- Supernatural coercion.
- Treating access to another person as an aesthetic need.

Vivienne is frequently the reason Nia's worst impulses do not become irreversible.

---

## The First Encounter with Le Masque

Nia encountered Le Masque while feeding from a mortal who wore it upon warm skin.

The experience combined:

- Blood.
- Body heat.
- White fabric.
- Grapefruit.
- Lemon.
- Rose.
- Clean musk.
- The residual smell of sunlight upon skin and clothing.

For a Kindred confined to night and cold flesh, the fragrance produced an emotional shock.

It reminded Nia not merely of the sun as light, but of the sun as something stored temporarily within a living body and its clothes.

Nia purchased Le Masque.

Upon her own cold skin, at night, the fragrance remained beautiful but incomplete.

It could not reproduce what she had experienced.

Nia became obsessed with the missing part.

---

## From Fragrance to Perfumer

Nia investigated the creator of Le Masque.

Once she discovered Alec, the obsession transferred.

Alec possessed everything the fragrance denied Nia:

- Warm skin.
- Breath.
- Daylight life.
- Creative discipline.
- A living relationship with scent.
- The ability to place emotion into fabric and skin without supernatural power.

Nia became smitten before meeting her.

She asked Vivienne to approach Maison Le Veil through corporate channels.

Vivienne agreed.

The investment was the door through which Nia entered Alec's life.

---

## What Nia Wanted

Nia wanted Alec as an unstable combination of:

- Protégée.
- Lover.
- Creative obsession.
- Source of warmth.
- Artistic collaborator.
- Living vessel for fragrance.
- Person whose attention Nia could monopolise.

Nia did not initially want Alec Embraced.

Alec's mortality was part of the fascination.

Nia loved:

- Alec's warmth.
- Fragrance blooming upon living skin.
- The ability to feed from her.
- The contrast between Alec's discipline and bodily vulnerability.
- The fact that Alec could still experience daylight.

Eternity would preserve Alec.

It would also destroy part of what Nia desired.

---

## Taking Alec's Nights

Nia gradually occupied Alec's nights.

She wanted:

- Dinners.
- Drives.
- Private fittings.
- Long conversations.
- Fragrance testing.
- Sex.
- Feeding.
- Alec's undivided attention.

Alec began sleeping through much of the day.

Her mortal relationships and professional routines suffered.

Nia understood that Margot and Ayo existed.

She did not consider their claims equal to her own desire.

This was not because she hated them.

They were simply outside the emotional frame through which she viewed Alec.

---

## Feeding and Lingering Kiss

Nia fed from Alec.

She also used:

**Lingering Kiss**

The power deepened Alec's attachment to the feeding experience and compromised her ability to evaluate the relationship clearly.

Nia told herself several things:

- Alec desired her already.
- The power intensified rather than fabricated pleasure.
- Alec was happier when she stopped resisting.
- The relationship was temporary.
- Vivienne would prevent it from becoming dangerous.
- Alec would eventually understand.

These rationalisations do not absolve her.

When Alec later learned the truth, the discovery became one of the central violations in their relationship.

---

## Vivienne's Attachment to Alec

Vivienne initially approached Alec for Nia.

Nia noticed when Vivienne's interest became personal.

She was amused at first.

Then pleased.

Then possessive.

Nia had no objection to sharing Alec with Vivienne in principle.

She objected to the possibility that Vivienne might understand Alec in ways Nia did not.

Vivienne admired Alec's business judgement, discipline, and Ventrue qualities.

Nia admired Alec's warmth, creativity, pride, and resistance.

Together they consumed different parts of her life.

---

## Influence upon Masquerade

Nia pushed Alec toward a fragrance unlike La Rose Bleue Voilée and Le Masque.

She encouraged:

- Greater sweetness.
- Red fruit.
- Warmth.
- Decadence.
- Rose as seduction rather than cleanliness.
- Evening wear.
- Blood-red liquid.
- Advertising built around implication, masks, beds, and multiple desires.

Nia did not compose Masquerade.

Her influence shaped the direction in which Alec permitted the composition to move.

The fragrance became an accidental record of the world forming around Alec.

Its name, suggested by Vivienne, concealed a second meaning Alec could not yet understand.

---

## The Proposed Embrace

When Vivienne and Nia realised that both had become too attached for Alec to remain a temporary phase, Vivienne proposed an eventual Embrace.

Nia resisted.

Vivienne argued:

- Alec would age.
- Alec would die.
- Their secrets would eventually require control.
- Alec possessed the qualities of a Ventrue.
- Ghouling would allow preparation.
- An authorised Embrace would preserve her permanently.

Nia eventually accepted.

They obtained permission from the Prince of Paris.

The plan was to:

1. Ghoul Alec.
2. Reveal Kindred existence gradually.
3. Teach her for at least one year.
4. Protect her.
5. Control her.
6. Allow Vivienne to Embrace her after preparation.

Nia accepted the plan because losing Alec entirely became more frightening than losing Alec's warmth.

Neither woman treated Alec's consent as the first requirement.

---

## The Assassination and Premature Embrace

Céleste Reine's assassination attempt destroyed the plan.

Vivienne saved Alec from final death by Embracing her after the shooting.

Nia was not present at the parking area.

Vivienne informed her afterward.

Nia's immediate emotions included:

- Relief.
- Rage.
- Grief for Alec's mortality.
- Jealousy that Vivienne performed the Embrace alone.
- Hatred toward the unknown attacker.
- Excitement that Alec would survive.
- Fear that Alec would blame them.

All of those fears were justified.

---

## Alec's Rejection

Once Alec understood:

- The feeding.
- Lingering Kiss.
- Vivienne's original purpose.
- The planned ghouling.
- The authorised future Embrace.
- The supernatural manipulation surrounding her business and nights.

she lashed out against both women.

Nia was furious.

She experienced Alec's anger as rejection after everything Nia believed they had shared.

Her first instinct was to force proximity, explain through sensation, and make Alec remember the pleasure between them.

Vivienne stopped her.

Vivienne argued that Alec was justified and that pressure would destroy any possibility of future reconciliation.

Nia accepted the restraint because she trusted Vivienne and because she feared making Alec hate her permanently.

---

## Current Relationship with Alec

The relationship is strained but not severed.

Nia and Alec maintained enough cordiality for Alec to learn the practical realities of Kindred existence.

Nia taught through:

- Sensory demonstration.
- Social observation.
- Feeding instruction.
- Toreador cultural knowledge.
- The dangers of desire within Kindred society.
- Lessons concerning performance and attention.

Alec never accepted Nia's account of their relationship as sufficient.

Nia has not accepted that love can be real and still become abuse.

Their attraction remains dangerous because neither woman can dismiss it as false.

---

## Saint Veyre

Nia and Vivienne once lived in Saint Veyre for approximately a decade.

Nia has memories, former lovers, artistic contacts, enemies, and unfinished relationships there.

Alec chose Saint Veyre partly through Vivienne's recommendation.

For Nia, the choice carries several meanings:

- Alec fled to a city Nia once called home.
- Alec accepted Vivienne's guidance while rejecting their proximity.
- Saint Veyre contains people who remember Nia from earlier decades.
- Returning could look like pursuit even if Nia has another reason.
- Remaining away requires patience Nia does not naturally possess.

Nia has not yet followed Alec.

That restraint should matter.

---

## Hidden Vulnerability

Nia's deepest wound is the loss of warmth.

Le Masque can trigger:

- Hunger.
- Nostalgia.
- Grief.
- Sensory fixation.
- Memories of sunlight.
- Memories of feeding from Alec.
- Anger at the Embrace she eventually accepted.

Nia also fears that Vivienne will one night stop being the constant.

She controls the relationship partly because control disguises dependence.

A credible threat to Vivienne may make Nia more reckless than a threat to herself.

---

## Long-Term Desire

Nia wants Alec back in her life.

She does not necessarily require the previous relationship to be restored exactly.

She does require proof that what they shared mattered.

Her desired future contains some combination of:

- Artistic collaboration.
- Romance.
- Feeding.
- Patronage.
- Acknowledged influence.
- A place beside Vivienne.
- Forgiveness without complete surrender of pride.

Nia's growth requires accepting that Alec may never offer all of these.

---

## Relationship to Vivienne

Nia loves Vivienne more consistently than she loves anyone.

Vivienne is:

- Partner.
- Restraint.
- Co-conspirator.
- Political shield.
- Emotional home.
- The person Nia trusts to tell her when desire has become destruction.

Nia may rage against Vivienne's restraint.

She usually follows it.

Alec is one of the few subjects capable of creating lasting conflict between them.

---

## Storyteller Notes

Nia should not be reduced to a predatory seductress, a jealous lover, or a glamorous monster.

She should be:

- Genuinely artistic.
- Genuinely loving.
- Genuinely selfish.
- Capable of delight and violence.
- More emotionally transparent than Vivienne.
- Less morally disciplined.
- Frightening because she understands feelings rather than because she lacks them.

Her relationship with Alec must preserve both truths:

**Alec desired her.**

**Nia violated Alec's autonomy.**

Neither truth cancels the other.

Nia's visual presence should remain warm, modern, intimate, and luxurious rather than conventionally gothic.
