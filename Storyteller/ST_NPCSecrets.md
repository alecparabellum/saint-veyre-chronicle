# Major NPC Secrets

## Purpose

This document records hidden motivations, vulnerabilities, ambitions, fears, and concealed relationships possessed by major recurring NPCs.

These secrets are unknown to most residents of Saint Veyre.

Some are known by a handful of individuals.

Some are known only by the NPC themselves.

The document should be updated whenever major revelations occur or important new NPCs are introduced.

Although the file is centred upon Saint Veyre, it also includes external figures whose secrets can materially affect the chronicle.

Detailed histories remain in the relevant character dossiers.

This file is an index of the truths most likely to shape future play.

---

# Prince Augustus Voss

## Public Persona

A stable and respected ruler committed to preserving Saint Veyre.

## Private Truth

Voss increasingly fears becoming irrelevant.

He remembers a city that no longer exists.

Many of the methods that secured his power centuries ago are losing effectiveness.

His greatest concern is not rebellion.

It is gradual obsolescence.

## Hidden Vulnerability

He occasionally delays necessary decisions while attempting to preserve political balances that may no longer be sustainable.

Several observant rivals have begun noticing.

## Long-Term Desire

To leave behind a stable legacy that survives his eventual departure.

---

# Seneschal Eleanor Whitlock

## Public Persona

Efficient administrator and loyal servant of the court.

## Private Truth

Whitlock's loyalty belongs first to Saint Veyre itself.

Only secondarily to individual rulers.

She has quietly served multiple political eras and has survived by prioritising continuity above personal allegiance.

## Hidden Vulnerability

Her commitment to institutional stability occasionally causes her to tolerate dangerous individuals if she believes removing them would create greater instability.

## Long-Term Desire

To ensure the city survives regardless of who occupies positions of power.

---

# Sheriff Marcus Vale

## Public Persona

A disciplined enforcer respected for competence and professionalism.

## Private Truth

Vale is exhausted.

He has spent decades containing problems that cannot truly be solved.

He privately believes Saint Veyre is approaching a period of significant change.

## Hidden Vulnerability

His sense of duty frequently overrides self-interest.

This makes him predictable to those who understand him.

## Long-Term Desire

To identify a successor capable of protecting the city after him.

---

# Helena Duvall

## Public Persona

Influential Harpy and social authority.

## Private Truth

Helena carefully cultivates the image of knowing everything.

She does not.

The illusion itself is her greatest source of power.

## Hidden Vulnerability

She fears public embarrassment more than physical danger.

A sufficiently humiliating revelation could damage decades of accumulated influence.

## Long-Term Desire

To remain indispensable regardless of changing political leadership.

---

# Adrian Moreau

## Public Persona

Elegant patron of culture, refinement, and tradition.

## Private Truth

Moreau's public support for tradition conceals deep dissatisfaction with the current political order.

He believes Saint Veyre is becoming stagnant.

## Hidden Vulnerability

His frustration occasionally leads him to support risky initiatives that contradict his carefully cultivated reputation.

## Long-Term Desire

To reshape the city without being seen as responsible for the disruption.

---

# Lucien Graves

## Public Persona

Pragmatic businessman and influential financial actor.

## Private Truth

Graves views politics primarily as resource management.

Ideology interests him very little.

## Hidden Vulnerability

He consistently underestimates emotional motivations.

He expects rational self-interest and is often surprised when others act otherwise.

## Long-Term Desire

To expand his influence into sectors currently beyond his control.

---

# Beatrice Laurent

## Public Persona

Scholar, historian, and respected intellectual voice.

## Private Truth

Laurent has spent decades quietly investigating historical inconsistencies surrounding Saint Veyre.

She possesses fragments of information many elders would consider dangerous.

## Hidden Vulnerability

Curiosity.

She occasionally pursues questions that would be wiser to leave unanswered.

## Long-Term Desire

To uncover a historical truth she suspects exists but cannot yet prove.

# Julian Ashcroft

## Public Persona

Charming political intermediary.

## Private Truth

Ashcroft maintains relationships across multiple factions.

Many assume he serves one side.

In reality he serves access itself.

## Hidden Vulnerability

His network depends upon trust.

Exposure of certain relationships could collapse years of careful work.

## Long-Term Desire

To become impossible to exclude from major political decisions.

# Vivienne Rousseau

## Public Persona

A refined and influential Ventrue patron whose wealth, restraint, and corporate judgement make her appear reassuringly competent.

She is Alec Le Veil's sire and Nia Angelou's long-term partner.

## Private Truth

Vivienne first approached Alec because Nia wanted access to the perfumer behind Le Masque.

Vivienne later developed genuine admiration and affection for Alec and concluded that she would make an exceptional Ventrue.

Vivienne and Nia obtained authorisation from the Prince of Paris for a future Embrace.

Their intended process was to ghoul Alec, educate her for at least one year, protect her, control her, and then allow Vivienne to Embrace her.

The assassination attempt forced Vivienne to perform the Embrace immediately.

Vivienne saved Alec from final death.

She also participated in planning Alec's future without Alec's knowledge or consent.

## Hidden Vulnerability

Vivienne believes careful planning and benevolent intention can make coercion morally acceptable.

Alec's rejection threatens Vivienne's identity as a reasonable and comparatively humane Ventrue.

Vivienne also knows more about the parking-area attack than almost anyone, but does not know who ordered it.

## Long-Term Desire

To see Alec become politically independent, commercially secure, and capable of surviving Kindred society.

Vivienne wants reconciliation without losing Nia and without admitting that Alec may owe her nothing.

# Nia Angelou

## Public Persona

A former fashion and show-business creative, private patron, established Toreador, and the emotionally dominant half of her long relationship with Vivienne Rousseau.

Nia is warm, charismatic, artistic, and visibly intense.

## Private Truth

Nia became obsessed with Le Masque after encountering it upon the warm skin of a mortal while feeding.

The fragrance reminded her of sunlight stored in skin and fabric.

Her obsession with the fragrance became obsession with Alec.

Nia wanted Alec as a lover, protégée, creative fixation, source of warmth, and living vessel for fragrance.

She fed from Alec and used Lingering Kiss.

Nia did not initially want Alec Embraced because Alec's warmth and mortality were central to the attraction.

She later accepted Vivienne's plan after the possibility of losing Alec became more frightening than losing Alec's mortal warmth.

## Hidden Vulnerability

Le Masque can bypass Nia's composure through hunger, grief, remembered sunlight, and memories of Alec.

Nia also depends upon Vivienne's constancy more deeply than she admits.

A credible threat that Vivienne may finally leave can make Nia reckless.

## Long-Term Desire

To regain a place in Alec's life and prove that their relationship mattered.

Nia wants some mixture of artistic collaboration, romance, feeding, acknowledged influence, and forgiveness.

She has not fully accepted that Alec may choose none of them.

# Céleste Reine

## Public Persona

The disciplined founder and chief executive of C'est La Reine.

She is admired for strategic intelligence, luxury-brand stewardship, and the ability to identify talent.

## Private Truth

Céleste genuinely liked Alexandra Jean and expected her to remain within C'est La Reine as the leader of its masculine division.

Alec's departure transformed pride into humiliation and commercial fear.

Céleste escalated through:

- False investors.
- Planted tabloid stories.
- Retail pressure.
- Restrictive model contracts.
- A failed blue-fragrance response.
- Coordinated supply-chain interference.
- Attempts to force Maison Le Veil reformulations.

When Vivienne quietly defeated the corporate sabotage, Céleste authorised Alec's assassination through a fixer and security intermediary.

Céleste is mortal.

She does not know Alec became Kindred.

## Hidden Vulnerability

Céleste never received a body.

She does not know whether Alec survived, died while Vivienne fabricated signs of life, or escaped after the attack.

The corporate fixer retained enough records to protect themselves.

Céleste therefore remains vulnerable to exposure, extortion, and the possibility that Alec knows the truth.

## Long-Term Desire

To preserve C'est La Reine, end or neutralise the royalty burden, contain Maison Le Veil, confirm Alec's status, and bury the conspiracy.

Céleste wants to believe the assassination order was an aberration rather than the logical result of her need for control.

# Ayo Musaku

## Public Persona

A young French perfumer of Nigerian descent whose early development was guided by Alec Le Veil.

She is Margot Musaku's younger sister.

## Private Truth

Ayo witnessed part of Le Masque's development and fell in love with Alec through artistic admiration, intimate mentorship, and sexual attraction.

Alec discussed placing Ayo at the creative centre of a future Maison Le Veil feminine division.

The plan never became formal.

Ayo possesses exercises, formula revisions, early feminine-division concepts, and private correspondence from the mentorship.

She believes Alec is alive.

She also believes something happened before Alec chose to withdraw.

## Hidden Vulnerability

Ayo fears that her talent will always be attributed to Alec, the affair, favouritism, or pity.

Recognition from someone she admires can bypass her caution.

She remains vulnerable to offers promising legitimacy, especially from Maison Le Veil, C'est La Reine, or another prestigious house.

## Long-Term Desire

To become a respected perfumer under her own name.

She wants reconciliation with Margot and the truth about Alec, but does not yet understand what either may cost.

# Margot Musaku

## Public Persona

A French secondary-school chemistry teacher, Alec's first girlfriend, and one of the few people who knew Alexandra Jean before the public persona of Alec Le Veil.

## Private Truth

Margot does not believe Alec is dead.

She believes Alec became too distant, famous, ashamed, or absorbed by another life and deliberately abandoned the Musakus.

Margot noticed Alec becoming nocturnal, exhausted, secretive, and dependent upon Vivienne's advice before the affair was exposed.

She preserves old correspondence, scent strips, an unfinished fragrance, a first-batch Rose Blue sample, messages from Alec's final Parisian period, and a card bearing Vivienne's corporate details.

Margot has no knowledge of Nia.

## Hidden Vulnerability

Margot remains protective of Ayo despite their damaged relationship.

Her deepest buried fear is that Alec did not leave freely.

If evidence proves that Alec was endangered or controlled, Margot must confront the possibility that she mistook victimisation for arrogance while still preserving the truth of Alec's betrayal.

## Long-Term Desire

To build a life in which Alec's choices no longer define the Musaku family.

Margot wants her loyalty to have meant something, but will not accept selective confession or supernatural influence as substitutes for accountability.

# Cross-Character Secret Network

## Who Knows an Assassination Attempt Occurred

- Alec, as the victim.
- Vivienne, who intervened.
- Nia, who was informed by Vivienne.
- Céleste, who authorised it.
- The corporate fixer.
- The security intermediary.
- The assassin.

The fixer, intermediary, and assassin possess different portions of the truth.

Margot and Ayo do not know an attack occurred.

The mortal public does not know.

## Who Knows Céleste Ordered It

- Céleste.
- The corporate fixer.

The intermediary and assassin know the operation but not the full corporate motive.

Alec, Vivienne, and Nia do not currently know Céleste was responsible.

## Who Knows Alec Became Kindred

- Alec.
- Vivienne.
- Nia.
- The limited Parisian Camarilla authorities required to regularise the already authorised Embrace.

Céleste, Margot, Ayo, the mortal public, and Maison Le Veil's ordinary employees do not know.

## Who Knows About the Planned Ghouling and Eventual Embrace

- Vivienne.
- Nia.
- The Prince of Paris and whatever limited authority processed the permission.
- Alec, but only after her premature Embrace.

Margot and Ayo do not know.

## Who Knows About Nia's Use of Lingering Kiss

- Nia.
- Alec, after learning enough to understand what had happened.
- Vivienne, who knows the relationship and its methods.

This should remain one of the most sensitive truths in any future confrontation.

## Who Knows Nia Was Central to Alec's Final Mortal Months

- Alec.
- Vivienne.
- Nia.
- Ayo saw Nia once or twice but did not understand her significance.

Margot does not know Nia existed.

## Who Knows About Céleste's Corporate Sabotage

No one outside the conspiracy knows the complete sequence.

Alec and Vivienne recognised coordinated interference.

They did not prove Céleste controlled it.

Fragments remain within corporate, supplier, public-relations, and financial records.

# The Court As A Whole

## Public Belief

The court is unified beneath the Prince.

## Private Reality

The court remains functional largely because most participants fear open conflict more than they dislike one another.

The appearance of unity often masks active competition.

# Future Additions

Whenever a major recurring NPC enters the chronicle, add:

- Public Persona.
- Private Truth.
- Hidden Vulnerability.
- Long-Term Desire.

Every major character should possess at least one secret.

Not every secret must be world-shaking.

Some should be political.

Some personal.

Some romantic.

Some financial.

Some historical.

The goal is not complexity for its own sake.

The goal is ensuring every important character possesses an internal life beyond what they present publicly.
