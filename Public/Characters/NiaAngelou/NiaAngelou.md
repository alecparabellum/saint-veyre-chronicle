# Nia Angelou

## Basic Information

**Full Name:** Nia Angelou  
**Clan:** Toreador  
**Nationality:** American  
**Apparent Age:** Early Forties  
**Occupation:** Former Fashion Designer, Costume Creative, and Show-Business Figure  
**Current Role:** Private Patron and Established Kindred  
**Primary Residence:** France  
**Status:** Established Kindred  

---

## Appearance

Nia is a dark-skinned Black woman with a striking, mature beauty and an unmistakable sense of physical ease.

She has:

- A long oval face.
- Deep-set dark eyes.
- High cheekbones.
- A broad, elegant nose.
- Full lips.
- Smooth dark-brown skin.
- Long limbs.
- A softly curvaceous, athletic figure.
- A calm gaze that can become intensely focused without warning.

Her most recognisable feature is her enormous natural 4C hair.

Nia wears it in a vast, dense, cloud-like shape that frames her face and shoulders. The style is luxurious, dramatic, and intentionally difficult to ignore.

Her clothing often combines comfort with private luxury.

Common elements include:

- White and ivory wrap blouses.
- Deep navy skirts.
- Gold sandals.
- Fine gold hoops.
- Simple medallion necklaces.
- Silk robes with dark piping.
- Bare feet within private interiors.
- Clothing that moves easily while still appearing expensive.

Nia's beauty does not depend upon formality.

She can look as composed in a robe upon a sofa as another person might at a gala.

---

## Personality

Nia is passionate, intense, charismatic, selfish, emotionally perceptive, and capable of overwhelming attention.

When interested in someone, she makes them feel singular.

When bored, her withdrawal can feel like a room losing heat.

Nia is not patient with emotional distance.

She prefers:

- Immediate feeling.
- Sensory experience.
- Art that provokes a physical response.
- People who surprise her.
- Desire acknowledged rather than politely concealed.
- Beauty that remains imperfect enough to feel alive.

She can be affectionate, playful, generous, and deeply inspiring.

She can also be possessive, impulsive, manipulative, and violent when denied something she has begun to regard as hers.

---

## Mortal Career

Before withdrawing from public mortal life, Nia worked in fashion, clothes design, costume, and show business.

Her career moved between:

- Wardrobe design.
- Stage clothing.
- Editorial styling.
- Private commissions.
- Performance costumes.
- Creative direction.
- Connections between clothing, movement, music, and persona.

Nia developed a reputation for understanding how fabric changes the way a person occupies space.

She was less interested in conventional elegance than in transformation.

Her designs often made the wearer feel more dangerous, sensual, or visible than they had expected.

She eventually disappeared from ordinary public life.

Within Kindred society, she continued influencing artists, designers, performers, and patrons through private networks.

---

## Reputation Among Kindred

Nia is known within French Kindred society as:

- A Toreador of strong appetite.
- A former fashion and show-business creative.
- A patron capable of advancing artistic careers.
- A woman whose fascinations can become consuming.
- The long-term partner of Vivienne Rousseau.
- The emotionally dominant half of one of France's established Kindred power couples.

Vivienne possesses greater formal influence.

Nia possesses greater control over the relationship itself.

Their bond has endured for decades despite operating openly rather than exclusively.

---

## Relationship to Vivienne Rousseau

Nia and Vivienne have known and loved one another for decades.

Their relationship became open after it became clear that exclusivity would eventually destroy it.

Vivienne initially resisted this arrangement.

Over time, she accepted that she remained the constant in Nia's unlife even when Nia pursued other lovers, protégés, and obsessions.

Nia trusts Vivienne more than she trusts almost anyone.

She also relies upon Vivienne to provide restraint, judgement, and stability when passion begins replacing proportion.

---

## Relationship to Alec Le Veil

Nia first became interested in Alec through her work.

She developed an intense attachment to:

**Le Masque**

Her fascination with the fragrance led her to its perfumer.

Nia became smitten with Alec's talent, warmth, confidence, and creative discipline.

Their relationship eventually became intimate.

Nia influenced the development of Masquerade by encouraging Alec toward:

- Greater sweetness.
- Red fruit.
- Warmth.
- Decadence.
- Evening wear.
- Blood-red presentation.
- A more openly sensual campaign identity.

Alec and Nia's relationship is now strained.

Affection and attraction remain present beneath anger, disappointment, and unresolved questions of control.

## Aesthetic Preferences

Nia gravitates toward:

- Natural hair treated as sculpture.
- White fabric against dark skin.
- Deep navy.
- Warm wood.
- Gold jewellery.
- Bare skin and bare feet in private spaces.
- Modernist interiors.
- Large tropical plants.
- Fragrance on warm fabric.
- Clothing that suggests intimacy without looking careless.
- Art that preserves evidence of the body which made it.

Her preferred spaces are luxurious without being ceremonial.

She dislikes interiors that feel designed for people to behave correctly inside them.

## Current Status

Nia remains based primarily in France.

She maintains her long relationship with Vivienne Rousseau.

Her relationship with Alec is cordial only in the broadest sense.

Nia has respected Alec's demand for distance more than many who know her would have expected.

Whether that restraint represents growth, strategy, or the patience of an obsession not yet finished remains uncertain.
