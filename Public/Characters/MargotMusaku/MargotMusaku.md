# Margot Musaku

## Basic Information

**Full Name:** Margot Musaku  
**Nationality:** French  
**Heritage:** Nigerian  
**Age:** Thirty  
**Occupation:** Secondary School Chemistry Teacher  
**Residence:** France  
**Status:** Mortal

---

## Appearance

Margot is a dark-skinned Black French woman with a composed, quietly striking presence.

She has a long oval face, thoughtful dark eyes, a broad nose, full lips, and a small natural gap between her front teeth. Her expressions tend to be restrained, but her face becomes considerably warmer when she smiles.

Her natural hair is most often worn in neat twists, a low braided bun, or another practical style that remains secure during laboratory demonstrations and long school days. She occasionally wears thin-framed glasses while reading or preparing lessons.

Margot has a slender-to-average build and carries herself with an upright, self-contained posture. Her wardrobe is practical and understated, favoring soft knitwear, buttoned blouses, tailored trousers, long skirts, comfortable shoes, and the occasional sharply cut coat.

She wears fragrance sparingly. What she does wear tends to be subtle, clean, and close to the skin.

---

## Personality

Margot is intelligent, patient, principled, and emotionally disciplined.

She is an attentive listener who prefers precise language and becomes frustrated when people use charm to avoid answering direct questions. Her humor is dry, occasionally sharp, and usually delivered with an expression so composed that people require a moment to realize she has made a joke.

Margot values reliability more than spectacle.

She is capable of profound loyalty, but betrayal is not something she dismisses merely because time has passed. Forgiveness, to Margot, requires honesty, accountability, and changed behavior.

She is not cold.

She is careful.

---

## Known History

Margot met Alexandra Jean while they were university students in France.

Their shared interest in chemistry brought them together before Alexandra fully entered the world of professional perfumery. Margot became Alexandra's first girlfriend and knew her before the professional identity of Alec Le Veil had eclipsed her birth name.

As their careers developed, their paths diverged.

Margot pursued education and eventually became a secondary school chemistry teacher. Alexandra entered the fragrance industry, built a public reputation, and increasingly became known as Alec Le Veil.

Margot supported Alec during the uncertain early stages of her career, before fame and commercial success transformed the scale of Alec's life.

Their relationship ended after Margot discovered that Alec had been sleeping with Margot's younger sister, Ayo.

The betrayal damaged all three relationships at once.

Margot lost her partner.

She also lost the uncomplicated trust she had once shared with her sister.

## Relationship to Alec Le Veil

Margot was Alec's first girlfriend and one of the earliest people to take her ambitions seriously.

She knew Alexandra before the wealth, the fashion coverage, the executive persona, and the mythology surrounding Alec Le Veil.

Their relationship was loving but increasingly strained by Alec's ambition, long working hours, growing fame, and appetite for attention.

The affair with Ayo ended the relationship decisively.

Margot's anger was not limited to sexual infidelity. She believed Alec had violated the loyalty of a partner, exploited the trust of a younger woman she was mentoring, and placed Margot between romantic betrayal and family fracture.

Alec's later disappearance and presumed death complicated emotions Margot had never fully resolved.

Grief did not erase her anger.

Anger did not erase the fact that Alec had once been the person she loved most.

---

## Relationship to Ayo Musaku

Ayo is Margot's younger sister.

Before the affair, Margot was protective of Ayo and took considerable pride in her creativity.

Their relationship became severely strained after Margot learned that Ayo and Alec had been sleeping together. The sisters did not sever contact completely, but the ease and trust between them disappeared.

Margot still feels responsible for Ayo's welfare, even when she resents that responsibility.

Their relationship remains wounded rather than dead.

---

## Public Perception

Margot is generally regarded as a demanding but fair teacher.

Students know that she expects preparation and honesty, but also that she will spend additional time helping anyone who genuinely wishes to understand the material.

Colleagues view her as dependable, discreet, and difficult to pressure into overlooking poor standards.

Few people in her current life know the full extent of her history with Alec Le Veil.

---

## Current Status

Margot continues to live and teach in France.

She has built a stable life that does not depend upon Alec's fame, money, or memory.

Nevertheless, Alec remains embedded in some of Margot's most formative experiences: first love, intellectual partnership, betrayal, grief, and the painful discovery that loyalty cannot be assumed merely because love once existed.
