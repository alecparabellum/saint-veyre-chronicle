# Margot Musaku

## Basic Information

**Full Name:** Margot Musaku  
**Nationality:** French  
**Heritage:** Nigerian  
**Age:** Thirty  
**Occupation:** Secondary School Chemistry Teacher  
**Residence:** France  
**Status:** Mortal  

---

## Appearance

Margot is a dark-skinned Black French woman with a composed, quietly striking presence.

She has a long oval face, thoughtful dark eyes, a broad nose, full lips, and a small natural gap between her front teeth.

Her expressions tend to be restrained, but her face becomes considerably warmer when she smiles.

Her natural hair is most often worn in neat twists, a low braided bun, or another practical style that remains secure during laboratory demonstrations and long school days.

She occasionally wears thin-framed glasses while reading or preparing lessons.

Margot has a slender-to-average build and carries herself with an upright, self-contained posture.

Her wardrobe is practical and understated, favouring:

- Soft knitwear.
- Buttoned blouses.
- Tailored trousers.
- Long skirts.
- Comfortable shoes.
- Modest jewellery.
- The occasional sharply cut coat.

She wears fragrance sparingly.

What she does wear tends to be subtle, clean, and close to the skin.

---

## Personality

Margot is intelligent, patient, principled, and emotionally disciplined.

She is an attentive listener who prefers precise language and becomes frustrated when people use charm to avoid answering direct questions.

Her humour is dry, occasionally sharp, and usually delivered with an expression so composed that people require a moment to realise she has made a joke.

Margot values reliability more than spectacle.

She is capable of profound loyalty, but betrayal is not something she dismisses merely because time has passed.

Forgiveness, to Margot, requires:

- Honesty.
- Accountability.
- Changed behaviour.
- Patience.
- Acceptance that forgiveness may not restore the previous relationship.

She is not cold.

She is careful.

---

## Known History

Margot met Alexandra Jean while they were university students in France.

Their shared interest in chemistry brought them together before Alexandra fully entered the world of professional perfumery.

Margot became Alexandra's first girlfriend and knew her before the professional identity of Alec Le Veil eclipsed her birth name.

As their careers developed, their paths diverged.

Margot pursued education and eventually became a secondary school chemistry teacher.

Alexandra entered the fragrance industry, built a public reputation, and increasingly became known as Alec Le Veil.

Margot supported Alec during the uncertain early stages of her career, before fame and commercial success transformed the scale of Alec's life.

Their relationship ended after Margot discovered that Alec had been sleeping with Margot's younger sister, Ayo.

The betrayal damaged all three relationships at once.

Margot lost her partner.

She also lost the uncomplicated trust she had once shared with her sister.

---

## La Rose Bleue Voilée

Margot loved La Rose Bleue Voilée on Alec.

The fragrance's clean rose, citrus, pepper, ambroxan, and after-shower musk became closely associated with Alec's presence.

Margot did not love the perfume because it was famous.

She loved the way it changed upon Alec's skin and clothing.

Encountering it unexpectedly can still produce a moment of recognition before memory catches up.

Margot rarely discusses this association.

---

## Vivienne Rousseau

Margot knew Vivienne only through Alec's descriptions.

Alec presented her as an extraordinary investor who:

- Understood Maison Le Veil.
- Shared Alec's values.
- Possessed exceptional corporate connections.
- Invested generously.
- Removed business obstacles.
- Treated Alec as someone worth taking seriously.

Margot never had enough direct contact with Vivienne to form an independent opinion.

She noticed only that Vivienne's arrival coincided with Alec becoming more distant, nocturnal, and difficult to reach.

At the time, Margot interpreted this as another stage in Alec's professional ascent.

---

## Relationship to Alec Le Veil

Margot was Alec's first girlfriend and one of the earliest people to take her ambitions seriously.

She knew Alexandra before:

- Wealth.
- Fashion coverage.
- The executive persona.
- Maison Le Veil.
- Public mythology.
- The name Alec became permanent.

Their relationship was loving but increasingly strained by Alec's ambition, long working hours, growing fame, and appetite for attention.

The affair with Ayo ended the relationship decisively.

Margot's anger was not limited to sexual infidelity.

She believed Alec had:

- Violated the loyalty of a partner.
- Exploited the trust of a younger woman she was mentoring.
- Placed Margot between romantic betrayal and family fracture.
- Used affection as though it excused unequal responsibility.

Margot still remembers Alexandra with tenderness.

She does not believe tenderness creates an obligation to forgive Alec.

---

## Alec's Withdrawal from Paris

During the final phase of their relationship, Alec became increasingly erratic.

She disappeared during the night, slept through much of the day, cancelled plans, and appeared absorbed by people and pressures Margot did not understand.

The discovery of the affair transformed concern into hurt and distance.

When Alec later withdrew from public life and apparently left Paris, Margot worried that something serious had happened.

Controlled signs of life eventually appeared.

Margot concluded that Alec was alive and deliberately avoiding the Musakus.

She came to believe that Alec had finally become too famous, too wealthy, and too consumed by her new life to remain connected to the people who had known Alexandra Jean.

This explanation hurt.

It was also easier to live with than uncertainty.

---

## Relationship to Ayo Musaku

Ayo is Margot's younger sister.

Before the affair, Margot was protective of Ayo and took considerable pride in her creativity.

Their relationship became severely strained after Margot learned that Ayo and Alec had been sleeping together.

The sisters did not sever contact completely, but the ease and trust between them disappeared.

Margot still feels responsible for Ayo's welfare, even when she resents that responsibility.

Their relationship remains wounded rather than dead.

## Public Perception

Margot is generally regarded as a demanding but fair teacher.

Students know that she expects preparation and honesty, but also that she will spend additional time helping anyone who genuinely wishes to understand the material.

Colleagues view her as dependable, discreet, and difficult to pressure into overlooking poor standards.

Few people in her current life know the full extent of her history with Alec Le Veil.

---

## Current Status

Margot continues to live and teach in France.

She has built a stable life that does not depend upon Alec's fame, money, or memory.

She does not currently believe Alec is dead.

She believes Alec chose distance.

Nevertheless, Alec remains embedded in some of Margot's most formative experiences:

- First love.
- Intellectual partnership.
- Ambition.
- Betrayal.
- Family fracture.
- Abandonment.
- The painful discovery that loyalty cannot be assumed merely because love once existed.
