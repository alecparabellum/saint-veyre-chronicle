# Alexandra "Alec" Le Veil

## Basic Information

**Birth Name:** Alexandra Jean  
**Current Name:** Alec Le Veil  
**Clan:** Ventrue  
**Generation:** 12th  
**Nationality:** French  
**Occupation in Mortal Life:** Perfumer, fragrance designer, entrepreneur  
**Current Status:** Neonate Kindred newly arrived in Saint Veyre  

---

# Early Life

Alexandra Jean was born into a humble French family.

She did not inherit wealth, influence, or prestigious connections.

Whatever opportunities she later enjoyed were earned through talent, discipline, calculation, and relentless work.

From a young age, Alexandra developed an unusual fascination with scent.

While most people experienced fragrance as a luxury or accessory, Alexandra viewed it as a language.

To her, perfumes were stories.

A scent could communicate confidence, desire, nostalgia, authority, vulnerability, or ambition without a single spoken word.

Driven by equal parts creativity and determination, Alexandra pursued formal education in chemistry, perfumery, and fragrance design, eventually studying near Versailles.

Even before her Embrace, she displayed many qualities Clan Ventrue would later admire:

- Intelligence.
- Ambition.
- Discipline.
- Leadership.
- Self-confidence.
- Commercial instinct.
- The ability to make others believe in a future she had not yet built.

She never aspired merely to succeed.

She aspired to matter.

---

# Margot Musaku

Alexandra met **Margot Musaku** while they were university students.

Their shared interest in chemistry brought them together before Alexandra entered the professional fragrance world.

Margot became Alexandra's first girlfriend.

She knew her before the wealth, public mythology, tailored velvet, magazine covers, and professional name.

Margot supported Alexandra through uncertainty.

She believed in Alexandra before belief became profitable.

Their relationship was loving, intellectually intimate, and increasingly strained by Alexandra's ambition, long working hours, appetite for attention, and rapid movement into a world Margot did not wish to inhabit.

Margot would later become one of Alec's Touchstones and the clearest origin of her conviction:

**Thou shalt be loyal to those who are loyal to you.**

Alec's betrayal of Margot remains the most painful contradiction in that conviction.

---

# Rise Within the Fragrance Industry

Upon graduation, Alexandra secured internships and junior positions with prominent fragrance houses.

Her talent quickly attracted attention.

She possessed a rare ability to combine artistic innovation with commercial viability, creating fragrances that appealed to both critics and consumers.

Her growing reputation brought her to the attention of:

**C'est La Reine**

The house was renowned for feminine fragrance but had repeatedly failed to establish a meaningful presence in the men's market.

Alexandra was brought in as an outside collaborator.

At twenty-five, she negotiated an unconventional contract.

Rather than accepting a standard payment, she demanded royalties.

Many considered the decision reckless.

Alexandra considered it confidence.

---

# La Reine de Versailles Pour Homme

The fragrance that transformed her career was:

**La Reine de Versailles Pour Homme**

The scent became an extraordinary success.

Sales exceeded every expectation.

The fragrance elevated C'est La Reine from a respected niche house to a major luxury player and established Alexandra as one of the most promising perfumers of her generation.

Because of her royalty agreement, Alexandra earned a substantial fortune.

For the first time in her life, she possessed both wealth and independence.

Alec remains profoundly proud of the fragrance.

She rarely speaks about it without reverence.

She avoids calling it her magnum opus, partly because she refuses to let her entire career be sealed beneath one achievement and partly because she still wants to believe her greatest work lies ahead.

The hesitation does not diminish what the fragrance means to her.

It was the moment her private talent became public power.

---

# Ayo Musaku

**Ayo Musaku**, Margot's younger sister, admired Alexandra's work and possessed a strong natural scent memory.

Alexandra recognised her potential and began teaching her:

- Raw material evaluation.
- Accord construction.
- Formula revision.
- Olfactory vocabulary.
- The relationship between visual mood and scent.
- The discipline required to turn instinct into repeatable craft.

Ayo's creativity differed from Alec's.

Where Alec tended to begin with structure, control, and strategic purpose, Ayo often began with emotion, colour, image, or memory.

Alec believed Ayo could become an exceptional perfumer if given discipline and confidence.

The mentorship became emotionally intimate and eventually sexual while Alec was still with Margot.

The affair was consensual and ethically compromised.

Alec was older, professionally established, Margot's partner, and responsible for Ayo's development.

Ayo's admiration was genuine.

So was Alec's attraction.

Neither fact made their conduct harmless.

---

# The Creation of Alec Le Veil

Following the success of La Reine de Versailles Pour Homme, Alexandra left C'est La Reine and founded her own fragrance company.

Around this time she adopted a new professional identity:

**Alec Le Veil**

The decision was not born from shame.

Alexandra remained proud of who she was and where she came from.

The new name was a brand, a public persona, and an expression of her belief that presentation carried power.

Over time, the distinction between Alexandra Jean and Alec Le Veil weakened.

The professional identity became the identity by which most people knew her.

---

# Founding Maison Le Veil

Maison Le Veil was created to prove that La Reine de Versailles Pour Homme had not been an isolated triumph.

The house combined modern French perfumery with classical elegance, emotional storytelling, and a visual identity built around masks, veils, and self-invention.

Its foundational series became:

**The Le Veil Collection**

The collection shared a structural backbone of grapefruit, rose, and musk while presenting radically different personalities.

The canonical release order was:

1. **Le Veil**, original first batch.
2. **La Rose Bleue Voilée Eau de Toilette.**
3. **La Rose Bleue Voilée Eau de Parfum.**
4. **Le Masque Eau de Parfum.**
5. **Masquerade Eau de Parfum.**
6. **Le Veil Limited Edition.**
7. **Masquerade Extrait de Parfum.**

---

# Creative Preferences

Alec personally wears masculine and unisex fragrance almost exclusively.

She likes feminine perfumes and enjoys smelling them on women, but they do not inspire her own composition work.

Her imagination gravitates toward:

- Woods.
- Smoke.
- Musk.
- Citrus.
- Rose stripped of conventional softness.
- Pepper.
- Ambroxan.
- Clean fabric.
- Restrained oud.
- Contrasts between polished brightness and dark texture.

Alec did not intend Maison Le Veil to exclude feminine perfumery forever.

She planned to create a dedicated feminine division under Ayo's creative direction.

Ayo would not merely assist Alec.

She would develop fragrances in the language Alec admired but did not naturally speak.

The plan never reached full production.

---

# La Rose Bleue Voilée

The first batch of the house signature was released as:

**Le Veil**

Reviewers quickly described it as a blue fragrance built around a masculine rose.

The phrase **blue rose fragrance** became viral.

Alec pivoted rapidly.

By the second batch, the fragrance had become:

**La Rose Bleue Voilée**

The liquid turned dusty blue in the Eau de Toilette and steel blue in the Eau de Parfum.

Blue roses entered the labels, cartons, and advertising.

The decision became one of the most commercially successful pivots of Alec's career.

La Rose Bleue Voilée became the cash cow of Maison Le Veil and the most direct commercial competitor to La Reine de Versailles Pour Homme.

The Eau de Toilette campaign used the same model from C'est La Reine's **Some Crowns Are Earned** advertisement.

The casting was widely interpreted as a provocation.

Alec never publicly confirmed that intention.

Margot loved the fragrance on her.

The clean rose, pepper, ambroxan, and post-shower musk became strongly associated with Alec in Margot's memory.

---

# Le Masque

Le Masque was the most intimate and emotionally precise fragrance Alec produced for Maison Le Veil.

It moved from cold grapefruit, lemon, juniper, and aldehydes into rose, white flowers, clean musk, cedar, oakmoss, and Orcanox.

Ayo witnessed part of its development.

Watching Alec translate an apparently simple idea into a layered emotional composition changed Ayo's understanding of perfumery.

It also made it impossible for Ayo to maintain emotional distance from her mentor.

Le Masque became the fragrance through which admiration matured into love.

Ayo considered La Reine de Versailles Pour Homme a masterpiece, but she did not enjoy wearing masculine perfume.

Le Masque was different.

It became her all-time favourite fragrance and a perfume she genuinely wore.

Within The Le Veil Collection, Le Masque is also Alec's favourite.

She is especially proud of its transition from cold citrus into warm linen, rose, and skin musk.

---

# Céleste Reine and the Corporate War

Céleste Reine originally liked Alexandra and underestimated the scale of her future independence.

Céleste accepted the royalty agreement because she expected Alexandra to remain at C'est La Reine and eventually govern its masculine division.

La Reine de Versailles Pour Homme's success delighted her.

Alexandra's departure did not.

Céleste felt shocked, disrespected, and threatened.

She initially hoped Maison Le Veil would fail and force Alec to return.

Instead, Alec made a series of strong decisions.

She rejected suspicious investors.

She pivoted Le Veil into La Rose Bleue Voilée.

She built a recognisable visual identity.

She created Le Masque.

She expanded her distribution.

Céleste responded through escalating covert interference.

The campaign included:

- Fake investors.
- Planted tabloid stories about Alec's promiscuity.
- Pressure upon retailers.
- Attempts to limit Maison Le Veil's access to stores.
- A late and unsuccessful C'est La Reine blue fragrance.
- Corporate collusion.
- Pressure upon major fragrance houses.
- Attempts to restrict access to raw materials.
- Plans intended to force reformulations of La Rose Bleue Voilée, Le Masque, and the developing Masquerade.

Alec recognised that someone was working against her.

She did not possess proof that Céleste controlled the full campaign.

---

# Nia Angelou

**Nia Angelou** was a Toreador with a former career in fashion, clothes design, costume, and show business.

She was intense, passionate, selfish, charismatic, and capable of obsession.

Nia encountered Le Masque while feeding from a mortal who wore it on warm skin.

The fragrance produced an olfactory echo of sunlight upon skin and fabric.

For a Kindred limited to night, cold flesh, and memory, the experience became unbearable in its beauty.

Nia bought the fragrance.

She could never make it behave upon her own skin as it had upon the mortal.

The perfume became an obsession.

The obsession became curiosity about its creator.

Curiosity became desire.

---

# Vivienne Rousseau

Nia had maintained a long open relationship with the Ventrue **Vivienne Rousseau**.

They had been lovers for decades and were regarded as a power couple within French Kindred society.

Vivienne possessed greater political and financial influence.

Nia controlled the emotional terms of the relationship.

Vivienne initially struggled with Nia's inability to remain exclusive.

Over decades, she accepted that she was the constant in Nia's unlife and that attempting to cage her would only result in losing her.

Vivienne approached Alec because Nia wanted access to her.

Using corporate connections, Vivienne became a generous Maison Le Veil investor.

Alec knew her as an intelligent, cultivated patron who shared many of her values and understood business unusually well.

Vivienne originally intended only to make Alec available to Nia.

Instead, she came to admire Alec's entrepreneurship, judgement, resilience, and instinct for authority.

Vivienne began to believe Alec would make an exceptional Ventrue.

---

# The Nights They Took

Nia wanted Alec as an unstable combination of:

- Protégée.
- Lover.
- Creative obsession.
- Source of warmth.
- Object of artistic possession.

She gradually occupied the entirety of Alec's nights.

Vivienne occupied the business hours around them, solving problems, offering counsel, and quietly removing corporate obstacles.

Alec slept through increasing portions of the day.

Her behaviour became erratic to the people who had known her before.

Margot and Ayo barely understood what was happening.

Margot never knowingly met Nia.

Ayo saw her once or twice after work but paid little attention to her.

They knew more about Vivienne, because Alec spoke of her as an extraordinary investor.

Neither understood that the investor and the barely noticed nocturnal woman had become the centre of Alec's nights.

Nia fed from Alec and used **Lingering Kiss** upon her.

The experience intensified Alec's attachment and further compromised her ability to understand the relationship clearly.

---

# The Musaku Fracture

During the same period, Margot discovered that Alec had been sleeping with Ayo.

Margot felt betrayed by both women.

She pulled back.

Alec's nocturnal disappearances, daytime sleeping, and increasingly distant behaviour made the betrayal feel like part of a larger abandonment.

Margot eventually concluded that Alec had become too famous, too wealthy, and too consumed by her new life to remain connected to them.

Ayo lost both her mentor and the emotional stability of her relationship with Margot.

Her affair with Alec had been exposed at exactly the moment Alec became least available to explain herself.

Ayo became increasingly worried when Alec disappeared for long periods.

Later signs of life calmed the immediate fear.

Ayo accepted that Alec was probably avoiding them, though she never fully believed that ordinary selfishness explained everything.

---

# Masquerade

Nia pushed Alec toward a warmer, sweeter, more decadent evening fragrance.

She wanted:

- Red fruit.
- Rose.
- Heat.
- Sweetness.
- Blood-red liquid.
- A perfume that behaved like seduction after midnight.

Vivienne influenced the business strategy surrounding the release and quietly removed the artificial supply restrictions created by Céleste's corporate pressure.

Vivienne also suggested the name:

**Masquerade**

To Vivienne, the name was an amused reference to the Camarilla's organising principle.

Alec knew nothing of the Masquerade and believed the name simply fitted Maison Le Veil's mask motif.

The resulting Eau de Parfum balanced tart red fruit, lush rose, amber, caramel, vanilla, musk, and restrained oud.

Alec considers it the correct version of the idea.

Masquerade Extrait was created later as a business response to customers demanding more oud, greater strength, and longer performance.

It is Alec's least favourite fragrance within the collection.

She believes the Extrait sacrifices part of the Eau de Parfum's journey by beginning too close to darkness.

---

# The Plan for Alec

Nia did not initially want Alec Embraced.

One of the things she loved most was Alec's warmth and the way fragrance developed upon living skin.

Vivienne was the first to imagine Alec as Kindred.

When both women recognised that their attachment was not temporary, they sought authorisation from the Prince of Paris.

The plan was to:

1. Make Alec a ghoul.
2. Reveal Kindred existence gradually.
3. Educate her for at least one year.
4. Protect her.
5. Control her.
6. Prepare her for an eventual Ventrue Embrace.

Nia accepted the plan despite her reluctance to lose Alec's mortal warmth.

Neither woman asked Alec what she wanted.

---

# The Assassination Attempt

Céleste's escalating desperation eventually became violence.

Alec was ambushed at night in an isolated parking area.

The attack was intended to kill her quietly and leave the event disconnected from the corporate war.

Vivienne was nearby.

She intervened against the assassin, but not before Alec had been shot.

The wounds were fatal.

Alec did not die instantly.

Vivienne had enough time to make one decision.

She Embraced Alec.

The planned year of preparation vanished.

Alec became Kindred without ghouling, education, informed consent, or warning.

---

# Aftermath

When Alec learned about Kindred society and understood the scale of Vivienne and Nia's manipulation, she lashed out against them.

She learned that:

- Nia had fed from her.
- Supernatural influence had shaped their intimacy.
- Vivienne had approached her for Nia.
- Vivienne had protected the company through methods Alec could not have recognised.
- Both women had planned her future without her consent.
- Permission for her eventual Embrace had already been obtained.

Nia was furious and devastated to lose Alec immediately after gaining the eternity she had reluctantly accepted.

Vivienne convinced her that Alec's anger was justified and that forcing proximity would deepen the rupture.

The three maintained a strained but functional relationship.

Alec needed education in Kindred society, feeding, law, politics, and survival.

Vivienne and Nia remained her principal teachers.

Cordiality never restored trust.

---

# Public Disappearance

The assassination attempt remained secret.

Céleste and the people involved in arranging it knew.

Vivienne knew.

Nia was informed by Vivienne.

The rest of the world did not know an attempt had occurred.

In the month following the Embrace, Alec became more reclusive.

Maison Le Veil representatives issued limited communications.

Alec eventually appeared to leave Paris.

She was not declared dead.

Controlled signs of life allowed the public, Margot, Ayo, business partners, and the press to conclude that she remained alive but had withdrawn from her previous life.

The ambiguity protected the Masquerade.

It also left the people Alec abandoned with no honest explanation.

---

# Saint Veyre

Vivienne and Nia had once spent approximately a decade of their unlives in Saint Veyre.

Vivienne described the city to Alec as a place where she could begin again without returning immediately to the political and emotional architecture of Paris.

Alec chose Saint Veyre partly because it offered distance from:

- Céleste.
- The corporate war.
- Margot.
- Ayo.
- Nia.
- Vivienne.
- The public identity of Alec Le Veil.
- The place where Alexandra Jean had effectively ceased to exist.

She did not arrive to disappear.

She arrived to rebuild.

---

# Relationship with Vivienne

Vivienne is Alec's sire.

Alec recognises that Vivienne saved her from final death.

She also recognises that Vivienne helped construct the situation in which Alec's choices had already been narrowed before the bullets arrived.

Their bond contains:

- Gratitude.
- Resentment.
- Admiration.
- Betrayal.
- Intellectual affinity.
- Familial obligation.
- Political expectation.
- Affection neither woman can dismiss cleanly.

Vivienne maintains that there was no alternative in the parking area.

Alec does not dispute the emergency.

She disputes everything that made the emergency the first moment in which her consent was treated as unnecessary.

---

# Relationship with Nia

Nia loved Alec intensely.

The intensity was real.

So was the selfishness.

Alec remembers warmth, pleasure, artistic intimacy, creative excitement, and the sensation of being desired without restraint.

She also remembers missing time, emotional dependence, supernatural influence, and the discovery that Nia had treated access to her life as something to be acquired.

Alec's feelings toward Nia remain volatile.

Desire has not erased anger.

Anger has not erased desire.

---

# Personality After the Embrace

Alec remains ambitious.

She desires influence, authority, recognition, and eventually domain.

She does not seek power merely to dominate others.

She refuses to remain powerless again.

Her mortal life taught her that talent could build freedom.

Her Embrace taught her that freedom without knowledge could be stolen in a single night.

Alec forms connections cautiously.

She is capable of loyalty and genuine affection.

Neither comes easily.

Her ambition drives her forward.

Her loneliness leaves her vulnerable.

---

# Current Goal

Alec arrives in Saint Veyre as an outsider.

She possesses no title.

No domain.

No standing within the local hierarchy.

She carries:

- Wealth.
- Intelligence.
- Ambition.
- Professional connections.
- Trauma.
- A surviving luxury house.
- A public identity she can no longer inhabit openly.
- Relationships she has not resolved.
- Questions about who ordered her death.
- Almost no practical experience of Kindred politics.

Saint Veyre is known as the City of Crowns.

Alec intends to claim one of her own.
