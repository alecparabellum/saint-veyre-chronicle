# Vivienne Rousseau

## Basic Information

**Full Name:** Vivienne Rousseau  
**Clan:** Ventrue  
**Nationality:** French  
**Apparent Age:** Late Forties to Early Fifties  
**Occupation:** Investor, Patron of the Arts, and Socialite  
**Primary Residence:** France  
**Former Residence:** Saint Veyre  
**Status:** Established Kindred  
**Childe:** Alec Le Veil  
**Long-Term Partner:** Nia Angelou  

---

## Appearance

Vivienne is a striking red-haired woman whose appearance combines mature refinement, artistic sensibility, and unmistakable authority.

Her hair is a rich copper-red, sometimes appearing deeper auburn beneath low evening light. She wears it in elegant, controlled styles suited to the occasion: softly structured waves, a polished swept-back arrangement, or a formal updo that exposes the line of her neck and the jewellery at her ears.

She has the composed beauty of a woman apparently in her late forties or early fifties.

Fine lines around her eyes and mouth give her expressions greater character rather than diminishing them. Her gaze is steady and attentive, and her face can appear warmly inviting without ever losing its underlying self-possession.

Vivienne's posture remains immaculate even in private settings.

Her movements are measured, but not rigid. She carries herself with the confidence of someone accustomed to being listened to without needing to demand it.

Her wardrobe commonly includes:

- Precisely tailored suits.
- Long structured coats.
- Silk blouses.
- Elegant evening gowns.
- Formal clothing softened by artistic or historical details.
- Ivory, charcoal, midnight blue, burgundy, forest green, and muted jewel tones.
- Fine gold jewellery.
- Luxury watches.
- Discreet heirloom pieces.
- Scarves, gloves, brooches, and accessories chosen for craftsmanship rather than visible branding.

Vivienne sometimes incorporates subtle references to art, culture, or history into her clothing.

She appears equally natural in a corporate boardroom, a private salon, a gallery opening, or a Camarilla gathering.

Nothing about her presentation looks accidental.

## Personality

Vivienne is intelligent, charismatic, patient, sophisticated, and highly controlled.

Her presence is warmer than the severity of many Ventrue.

She is capable of making people feel genuinely heard, often identifying the ambition, fear, or unspoken need beneath what they have chosen to say aloud.

Vivienne prefers to understand a problem before acting upon it and is rarely provoked into an immediate response.

Her manner can be reassuring because she does not waste movement, language, or attention. People often leave conversations with Vivienne feeling that a difficult situation has become more manageable, even when she has made no explicit promise.

Vivienne values:

- Competence.
- Long-term thinking.
- Loyalty.
- Discretion.
- Institutional stability.
- Self-command.
- Ambition supported by discipline.
- Artistic and intellectual curiosity.
- Influence exercised without spectacle.
- Beauty capable of surviving fashion.

By Kindred standards, she is reasonable and capable of genuine kindness.

That kindness should not be mistaken for softness.

Vivienne can be ruthless when she believes the situation requires it, particularly when protecting people, institutions, or plans she considers important.

## Reputation Among Kindred

Vivienne is a respected Ventrue with longstanding influence in France.

She is known as:

- A discerning patron of the arts.
- A sophisticated socialite.
- An experienced investor.
- A mentor to promising individuals.
- A woman with strong ties to artistic and intellectual circles.
- A political and social intermediary who rarely advertises the full extent of her reach.
- Someone capable of recognising exceptional potential before it becomes obvious to others.

Vivienne moves comfortably through:

- Finance.
- Luxury industry.
- Corporate law.
- Art patronage.
- Parisian cultural institutions.
- Galleries and private collections.
- Ventrue society.
- Camarilla political networks.
- Intellectual salons.
- Private philanthropic circles.

Her reputation rests partly upon the persistent impression that she possesses more options, contacts, and leverage than are immediately visible.

Among those who remember her time in Saint Veyre, she is regarded as cultured, influential, and difficult to place entirely within one social sphere.

## Patronage and Cultural Influence

Vivienne has supported artists, designers, scholars, institutions, and ambitious founders for many years.

She is drawn to individuals whose talent is real but whose position remains fragile.

Her patronage may involve:

- Direct financial investment.
- Private commissions.
- Introductions to collectors or institutions.
- Protection during professional controversy.
- Access to specialised expertise.
- Discreet social sponsorship.
- Strategic advice.
- Invitations into circles otherwise difficult to enter.

Vivienne does not treat patronage as charity.

She invests attention and resources where she believes talent, judgement, or ambition can become durable influence.

She is particularly interested in the point where artistic identity meets institutional power.

Vivienne understands that a beautiful object, successful company, public career, or cultural reputation can become a form of authority.

---

## Corporate Influence

Vivienne has invested in luxury, culture, and creative enterprise for many years.

Her business interests often overlap with her cultural interests.

She is particularly skilled at:

- Evaluating founders.
- Identifying durable brands.
- Negotiating complex agreements.
- Protecting companies during periods of instability.
- Connecting creative figures with capital and institutional access.
- Resolving corporate disputes through private channels.
- Recognising when an apparently artistic decision carries political or financial consequences.
- Preserving long-term control beneath short-term commercial pressure.

Vivienne prefers influence that remains elegant enough to be mistaken for good fortune.

## Relationship to Nia Angelou

Vivienne and the Toreador Nia Angelou have known and loved one another for decades.

They are regarded as one of the established power couples of French Kindred society.

Their relationship is openly non-exclusive.

Vivienne initially found this arrangement difficult, but eventually accepted that attempting to confine Nia would destroy the relationship rather than preserve it.

Vivienne possesses greater formal influence, financial power, and political reach.

Nia has greater control over the emotional terms of their relationship.

The two balance one another.

Vivienne provides:

- Patience.
- Restraint.
- Planning.
- Institutional memory.
- Political judgement.
- Stability.

Nia provides:

- Passion.
- Artistic instinct.
- Audacity.
- Social magnetism.
- Emotional momentum.
- The refusal to let caution become stillness.

Despite their differences and Nia's other attachments, Vivienne remains the constant presence in her unlife.

Their bond is affectionate, difficult, resilient, and widely understood to be more enduring than its unconventional structure first suggests.

---

## Relationship to Alec Le Veil

Vivienne first entered Alec Le Veil's mortal life as an investor in Maison Le Veil.

Alec knew her as an unusually intelligent and generous patron who understood luxury business, long-term strategy, and the importance of preserving creative control.

Vivienne became associated with:

- Corporate strategy.
- Investment.
- Supply relationships.
- Retail access.
- Crisis management.
- Cultural introductions.
- The protection of Maison Le Veil's independence.

Their professional relationship developed into genuine personal regard.

Vivienne admired Alec's entrepreneurship, judgement, resilience, creative discipline, and instinct for authority.

She recognised in Alec the qualities of someone capable of building not merely a successful fragrance house, but a lasting sphere of influence.

Vivienne later became Alec's sire.

The Embrace occurred under emergency circumstances and without the preparation either woman would have preferred.

Their relationship afterward became deeply strained.

Alec recognises that Vivienne saved her existence.

She also resents the secrecy, control, and unequal power surrounding their relationship.

Vivienne remains one of the most important figures in Alec's unlife, whether Alec welcomes that importance or not.

## Former Life in Saint Veyre

Vivienne and Nia once lived in Saint Veyre for approximately a decade.

During that period, Vivienne was known among the city's Kindred as a cultured and influential Ventrue with strong artistic, intellectual, financial, and social ties.

She was associated with:

- Cultural patronage.
- Private salons.
- Luxury institutions.
- Artistic commissions.
- Intellectual circles.
- Corporate introductions.
- Camarilla society.

Vivienne retains memories, contacts, and an informed understanding of Saint Veyre's political culture.

She later described the city to Alec as a place where an ambitious neonate might begin again outside the immediate political and emotional architecture of Paris.

Alec's decision to relocate there was partly influenced by Vivienne's knowledge of the city.

The exact extent of Vivienne's surviving Saint Veyre relationships is not widely known.

## Social Manner

Vivienne is courteous without becoming distant.

She listens carefully and rarely interrupts without purpose.

Her questions tend to be precise, and her compliments often identify a specific strength rather than offering general praise.

Vivienne is particularly skilled at making an ambitious person feel understood without making herself appear easily impressed.

She dislikes:

- Carelessness disguised as spontaneity.
- Public emotional theatrics.
- Wasteful cruelty.
- Poor preparation.
- Ambition unsupported by competence.
- People who confuse loudness with authority.
- Patronage used merely to purchase admiration.
- Cultural prestige without genuine curiosity.

Vivienne is difficult to intimidate because she seldom treats the immediate moment as the complete problem.

Her warmth is real.

So is the calculation beneath it.

## Current Status

Vivienne remains based primarily in France and continues her long relationship with Nia Angelou.

Her relationship with Alec is functional but unresolved.

Distance has prevented open conflict from consuming what remains between them, but it has not restored trust.

Vivienne continues to regard Alec as someone of exceptional potential.

Alec continues to regard Vivienne as both protector and architect of a life she did not choose.

Vivienne has not returned permanently to Saint Veyre.

Her former history there, her bond with Alec, her relationship with Nia, and her influence within French Kindred society ensure that her absence is not the same as irrelevance.
